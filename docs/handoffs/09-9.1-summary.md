# Summary — 9.1 Identity and design-system foundation

## Done
- `CategoryIdentity` (core): `displayName(hasBattery:)`, `shortLabel(hasBattery:)`, `symbol(hasBattery:)`, `liveSymbol(_:hasBattery:)` over `LiveSymbolState` (battery charge/charging, connection type, external volume, thermal peak, fanless). `MetricCategory.displayName/symbolName/shortLabel` forward to it. Power is "Battery" (default) / "Power" (desktop); GPU `cube.transparent`, Network `network`, Power `battery.100percent` / `powerplug`.
- `StatusLevel`: single mapping in core (`symbolName`, `symbolNameFilled`); `CategoryPalette` `iconName` forwards, `StatusRibbon` private mapping removed.
- Ad-hoc symbols replaced: Alerts and General badges use `SettingsBadge(category:)` (identity symbol and `CategoryPalette` tint; battery badges use `liveSymbol`); Fan empty states use `fan.slash` (was `wind`).
- Palette re-hued with one hue family per category across light/dark (see below). Core helpers `RGBColor.hueDegrees`, `.saturation`, `.hueDistance`.
- Tokens: `Tokens.swift` and its 4 pbxproj entries deleted. `Instrument.Typography` collapsed (removed `sectionTitle`, `control`; `micro` now 10 pt; added `captionMedium`, `captionStrong`, `headlineStrong`, `titleNumeric`, `icon(_:weight:)`, `Instrument.Icon` presets). Added `Space.sectionGap`, `Radius.chip/inline`, `Size.headerStripHeight/footerHeight/chartHeightCompact/Standard/Tall` (removed `chartHeight`, `historyChartHeight`, `eyebrowHeight`). `FilterChip` / `SegmentedControl` `fontSize` parameters removed.
- Sweep in `iStats/UI/Panels` and `iStats/UI/Instrument`: all literal `.font(.system(size:`, radii 4-8, `spacing:` and `.padding` literals on the 2/4/6/8/12/16/20/24 scale replaced by tokens; chart height 72/84/140 literals replaced.
- Thresholds: added `StatusThresholds.cpuCore`, `cpuLoad`, `cpuPanel`, `gpuPanel`, `gpuMemory` and chart-line constants; adopted in CPU, GPU, Memory, Temperature, Power panels and `BatteryGlyph`. Values preserved (panel tables are intentionally quieter than the menu bar `cpu`/`gpu` tables).

## Discrepancies / not done
- `xcodebuild test -scheme iStats` reports "no test bundles"; this is identical on the baseline commit. The test action lives in the `iStatsApp` scheme. The Xcode count (397) differs from SwiftPM (405 app tests); not investigated.
- Hue test exemptions: Fans by saturation; Temperature is exempt from the elevated/warning/critical status hues (orange sits inside the status arc). Every saturated category is >= 15 deg from the "fine" green.
- Behavior notes: History chips grow from 10.5 to 11 pt; sub-10 pt glyphs/text (8, 9, 9.5) are now 10 pt; radii 5 and 7 became 6; `Module`, `PanelFactory` and Temperature section titles moved from 11.5 to 11 pt semibold.
- `SymbolAvailabilityTests` shows `cube.transparent` resolves on the test host; no `display` fallback needed. Unverified on a real macOS 13 host. Charging uses `battery.100percent.bolt` for every level (no per-level bolt variants).
- Spacing/padding sweep covered only values on the Space scale (others such as 2.5, 3, 5, 7, 10, 14 remain literal). Per-category `symbolName(hasBattery:)` call sites in `PanelScaffold` still go through the `MetricCategory` forwarders (no battery knowledge there yet; 9.3 will pass it).
- Menu bar renderer palette/`.systemX` colors untouched (9.7). Commits 3 and 4 of the plan are combined (shared files).
