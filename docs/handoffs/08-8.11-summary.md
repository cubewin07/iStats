## 1. What was verified & implemented

Executed a comprehensive accessibility and appearance audit across all 8 panels (CPU, Memory, GPU, Temperature, Fans, Network, Disk, Power) and all view modes (Breakdown, History, Consumers) per [`design.md §7`](../phases/phase-08-premium-redesign/design.md) and [`tasks/8.11-accessibility-appearance-matrix.md`](../phases/phase-08-premium-redesign/tasks/8.11-accessibility-appearance-matrix.md):

1. **Appearance Matrix (Light & Dark Mode):**
   - Verified semantic color tokens in `CategoryPalette` and `Instrument.Surface`.
   - In Dark mode: dark translucent glass background with vibrant category tinting and WCAG AA contrast.
   - In Light mode: crisp light frosted glass with darkened text and adjusted stroke opacities ensuring readability against light or dark wallpapers.
   - Tested automated layout and size stability: all 8 panels render cleanly at exact 352 pt width without layout overflows or clipped labels.

2. **Differentiate Without Color:**
   - Added `symbolName` to `StatusLevel`:
     - `.nominal` → `checkmark.circle`
     - `.attention` → `info.circle`
     - `.warning` → `exclamationmark.triangle`
     - `.critical` → `exclamationmark.octagon`
   - Updated `MetricRow`: reads `@Environment(\.accessibilityDifferentiateWithoutColor)` to swap simple colored status dots for distinctive SF Symbols when enabled.
   - Updated `LiveChart`: reads `@Environment(\.accessibilityDifferentiateWithoutColor)` to apply distinct dashed strokes (`dash: [4, 3]`) on secondary/dual-series paths (e.g. Upload, Disk Write, System CPU) so curves do not rely solely on color distinction.

3. **High Contrast (`Increase Contrast`):**
   - Updated `Module`: reads `@Environment(\.colorSchemeContrast)` to add a crisp 1 pt outline (`Color.primary.opacity(0.2)`) *only* in Increased Contrast mode, strictly adhering to the design spec prohibition against gratuitous borders in standard mode.
   - Increased text and glyph contrast tokens across header eyebrows and secondary values.

4. **Reduce Transparency:**
   - In `MetricPanel` and `Instrument.Surface`: falls back gracefully from thin material / glass blur to opaque `windowBackgroundColor` with high contrast dividers so panel sections remain distinctly readable against any wallpaper.

5. **Reduce Motion:**
   - Integrated with `MotionPolicy.reduced` and `MotionPolicy.frozen`.
   - Verified that continuous ticker gliding, particle emitters, and spin animations immediately shut off, reverting to 0.15s clean opacity crossfades.
   - `LiveChartMath.glideOffset` safely returns `0.0` when motion is disabled or interval is invalid.

6. **VoiceOver & Text Scaling:**
   - Verified logical hierarchy across `PanelScaffold`:
     - Eyebrow / Panel title & Close button
     - Glance hero value & micro metadata
     - Chart interactive region
     - Page Switcher Ribbon (Breakdown · History · Consumers)
     - Page Body Content
   - Scaled numerals in `LiveNumber` and `PanelScaffold` with `.lineLimit(1)` and `.minimumScaleFactor(0.7)` so numerals never truncate under macOS 14+ accessibility text size preferences.
