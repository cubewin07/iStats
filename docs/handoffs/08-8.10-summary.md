## 1. What was built

Implemented instrument-grade History v2 page and components per [`design.md §6.9`](../phases/phase-08-premium-redesign/design.md) and [`tasks/8.10-history-v2.md`](../phases/phase-08-premium-redesign/tasks/8.10-history-v2.md):

1. **`HistoryTimeRange` & Preference Persistence (`Sources/iStatsCore/`):**
   - Added canonical `HistoryTimeRange` (`1m`, `5m`, `15m`) with duration math (`60s`, `300s`, `900s`) to `Sources/iStatsCore/Models.swift`.
   - Added `categoryHistoryRanges` dictionary and `historyRange(for:)` / `setHistoryRange(_:for:)` to `PreferencesStore.swift` for per-category persistence under ADR 0006 zero-telemetry rules.

2. **`LiveChart` Instrument Upgrades (`iStats/UI/Instrument/LiveChart.swift`):**
   - `showAxisTicks`: draws subtle dashed horizontal guidelines and trailing `niceTicks` labels formatted via `valueFormatter` with `Instrument.Typography.micro`.
   - `showPeakMarker`: renders ▲ peak marker with direct numerical readout positioned cleanly above or adjacent to the apex.
   - `showAverageLine`: renders 1 pt dashed average line across chart bounds.
   - `isolatedChannel`: applies dimming (0.15 opacity fill and stroke) to unselected channel for `.stacked` and `.mirrored` dual-series modes.
   - Interactive crosshair lens: displays snapped curve dot, vertical hairline, and floating lens badge showing relative time ("2m 14s ago") and formatted numerical value.
   - External scrub index binding: allows programmatic jump to peak, min, average, or now indices.

3. **`PanelScaffold` Chart Zone Coordination (`iStats/UI/Panels/PanelScaffold.swift`):**
   - Zone ③ glance chart collapses (`frame(height: 0)`) when `selection == .history` to prevent duplicate charts.
   - Injected `@Namespace private var chartNamespace` via `EnvironmentValues` for smooth `matchedGeometryEffect` transitions when entering and leaving History under full motion.

4. **`HistoryPage` View (`iStats/UI/Panels/HistoryPage.swift`):**
   - Glass segmented range control: `1m · 5m · 15m`.
   - 140 pt vector `LiveChart` with niceTicks, avg dashed line, ▲ peak marker with value, and relative-time lens.
   - Stats strip (Min · Avg · Max · Now) driven by `LiveNumber`, with interactive click-to-scrub crosshair jumps and haptic feedback.
   - Dual-series channel isolation chips:
     - CPU: User vs System
     - Network: Download ↓ vs Upload ↑
     - Disk: Read vs Write
   - Thermal sensor source selector chips (Peak, CPU, GPU, Memory, Storage).
   - Empty states for fanless systems and windows with < 10 samples ("Collecting History — history fills while iStats runs").
   - Wired across all 8 panels (`CPUPanel`, `MemoryPanel`, `GPUPanel`, `TemperaturePanel`, `FanPanel`, `NetworkPanel`, `DiskPanel`, `PowerPanel`) and `PanelFactory`.
