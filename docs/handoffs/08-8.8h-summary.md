## 1. Summary of Changes

1. **Pure Power Flow Calculation Engine (`PowerFlowMath`):**
   - Implemented `Sources/iStatsCore/PowerFlowMath.swift` and `PowerFlow` struct.
   - Evaluates 3-node power topology (`Adapter -> Mac`, `Adapter -> Battery`, `Battery -> Mac`) with net battery wattage derived from physical `voltageVolts * amperageMilliAmps / 1000.0`.
   - Normalizes particle speeds using log1p curve clamped to `[0.1, 1.0]`.
   - Fully covers charging, discharging, on-hold (optimized battery charging), power deficit, missing V/A, and desktop architectures.
   - Verified by 7 comprehensive unit tests in `Tests/iStatsCoreTests/PowerFlowMathTests.swift` (7/7 passed).

2. **Custom Battery Glyph (`BatteryGlyph`):**
   - Implemented `iStats/UI/Instrument/Kit/BatteryGlyph.swift` with an 88×40 pt custom enclosure and terminal nub.
   - Proportional charge fill with power tint, warning/critical status color transitions, and animated charging shimmer sweep (`MotionPolicy.full` only) centered with bolt glyph.

3. **3-Node Animated Power Flow Diagram (`PowerFlowView`):**
   - Implemented `iStats/UI/Instrument/Kit/PowerFlowView.swift`:
     - Adapter, Mac, and Battery interactive circular nodes with `LiveNumber` wattage displays.
     - Canvas rendering animated flowing particles driven by `TimelineView` at speeds calculated by `PowerFlowMath`.
     - Distinct visual paths for charging, discharging, and deficit assisting (with warning coral tint).
     - Degrades cleanly to static flow indicators under reduced motion.

4. **Dedicated Power Instrument Panel (`PowerPanel`):**
   - Implemented `iStats/UI/Panels/PowerPanel.swift`:
     - **Glance Header:** Charge percentage for battery Macs; live system wattage for desktop Macs. Mirrored by `PanelHeadline.power` time-to-full / time-remaining copy.
     - **Status Ribbon:** Automatically surfaces warnings on power deficit, low battery, or informs on-hold charging limits.
     - **Now Page:** Battery Glyph and Power Flow diagram for battery Macs; ArcGauge power draw for desktop Macs.
     - **Detail Page (Health):** RingStack maximum capacity gauge, charge cycle Meter, condition, and adapter telemetry (battery Macs only).
     - **Desktop Page Adaptability:** Automatically scales to 2 pages (`[.overview, .history]`) on desktop Macs without batteries.

5. **Integration & Wiring:**
   - Wired `PowerPanel` into `PanelFactory.makeCategoryPanelView` for `case .power`.
   - Updated `project.pbxproj` with `BatteryGlyph.swift`, `PowerFlowView.swift`, and `PowerPanel.swift`.

6. **Testing:**
   - Added `testPowerPanelBatteryMacRendersCleanly` and `testPowerPanelDesktopMacRendersCleanly` to `Tests/iStatsTests/PanelScaffoldTests.swift`.
