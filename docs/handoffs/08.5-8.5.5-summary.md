- **Step 8.5.5.1 — Scalar-Only Queries & Time-Aligned Envelopes (`iStats/UI/Panels/HistoryPage.swift`):**
  - Switched `.oneMinute` to `coordinator.timedScalarHistory(for:duration: 60)` and `coordinator.timedDualScalarHistory(primary:secondary:duration: 60)` (B8).
  - Switched `.fiveMinutes` (`bucketWidth: 2.5`) and `.fifteenMinutes` (`bucketWidth: 7.5`) to `coordinator.bucketedScalarHistory(for:duration:bucketWidth:)` and `coordinator.bucketedDualScalarHistory(primary:secondary:duration:bucketWidth:)`, passing `.envelope` / `.mirroredEnvelope` `BucketPoint` series and explicit `windowDuration` to `LiveChart`.
