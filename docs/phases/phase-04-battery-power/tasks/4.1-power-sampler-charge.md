# Task 4.1 — PowerSampler: charge / state / time remaining

**Phase:** 4 — Battery & Power
**Requirements:** 8.1

## What to do

Implement `PowerSampler` reading `IOPowerSources` for:

- Charge percentage.
- Charging / discharging / charged state.
- Time remaining when available.

## Goal (definition of done)

- Charge %, state, and time-remaining are reported and track `pmset -g batt`.
- Time-remaining is `nil` (not zero) when macOS is still calculating it.
