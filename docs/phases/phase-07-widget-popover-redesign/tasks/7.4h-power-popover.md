# Task 7.4h — Battery & Power Popover (`Overview · Health · History`) & Legacy Power View Removal

**Phase:** 7 — Widget & Popover Redesign (Sub-phase 7E: Category Popovers)
**Depends on:** 7.3c
**Parallel with:** 7.4a–g

## Purpose

*"How long will it last, is the charger enough, how healthy is it?"* — Replace the 6 legacy power popovers, `PowerSummaryView`, and `PowerBudgetIllustrationView` with a single tabbed `PowerPopover.swift`.

## What to do

1. **Step 7.4h.1 — Build `Overview` tab in `iStats/UI/Popovers/PowerPopover.swift`:**
   - **Battery Mac Hero:** `82%` + human state context (`"Charging · 38 min to full"`, `"On battery · 4 h 12 min left"`, or `"Charging on hold"` from `PowerStateVariant`).
   - **Power Flow Strip:** Numeric flow strip `Adapter 67 W → System 23 W → Battery +18 W` (or `Battery −14 W` when discharging). If `powerDrawWatts` exceeds `adapterWatts` while plugged in, highlight the deficit in status `.orange` with `"Draw exceeds adapter"`.
   - **Details:** Adapter name/wattage row + 60 pt wattage `TrendChart`.
   - **Desktop Mac (`hasBattery == false`, 2 tabs: `Overview · History`):** System power draw (`W`) as hero + `TrendChart` + adapter/power source info.
2. **Step 7.4h.2 — Build `Health` tab (Battery Macs only):**
   - **Maximum Capacity:** `currentMaxCapacity / designCapacity` percentage with `RangeBar`.
   - **Cycle Count:** `cycleCount` vs `designCycleCount` bar.
   - **Diagnostics:** Condition string, voltage (`V`), amperage (`mA`), and battery temperature (sourced from `coordinator.latestThermal` battery sensors via `ThermalSensorClassifier`).
3. **Step 7.4h.3 — Wire `PopoverFactory` and delete legacy Power views:**
   - Route `.power` in `PopoverFactory` to `PowerPopover` (footer context action opens `"Battery Settings"`).
   - Remove the 6 `Power*PopoverView` structs from `CategoryDetailPopoverView.swift`, and delete `PowerSummaryView.swift` and `PowerBudgetIllustrationView.swift`.
