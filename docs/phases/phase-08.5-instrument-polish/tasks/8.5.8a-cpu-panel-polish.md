**Phase:** 8.5 (Sub-phase 8.5C) · **Depends on:** 8.5.3, 8.5.7 · **Blocks:** 8.5.9

## Purpose
Polish `CPUPanel` to eliminate hover-scale gimmicks on `ClusterStrip`, give E-cores their own dedicated `cpuEfficiency` hue, replace the text-only Load Average row on Details with a true **Load Average Dot Plot** against core capacity, and arrange per-core sparklines into a compact 2-column grid.

## What to do
1. **Now Page (`iStats/UI/Panels/CPUPanel.swift`, `ClusterStrip.swift`):**
   - Feed timestamped `timedScalarHistory` for `.cpuUser` and `.cpuSystem` into the stacked hero `LiveChart`.
   - Remove `.scaleEffect(1.1)` on hover in `ClusterStrip`; highlight the hovered bar cleanly via opacity/border contrast and update the internal section title (`"Cores"` → `"Core 5 (P) · 74%"`).

   - Use `10×36 pt` rounded columns with labelled P/E cluster groups (`"Performance · 42%"`, `"Efficiency · 18%"`).
2. **Details Page:**
   - Replace the text-only `loadAverageModule` with a visual **Load Average Dot Plot**: a horizontal track scaled from `0` to `max(Double(coreCount) * 1.5, maxLoad * 1.1)` with a vertical threshold tick at `coreCount` (`"Capacity: \(coreCount)"`) and 3 plotted markers (`1m`, `5m`, `15m`) plus copyable tabular readout.
   - Replace the 1-column vertical list of all cores with a compact **2-column Per-Core Grid** (`LazyVGrid`, 2 columns, each tile showing core label, `32×12 pt` sparkline, and tabular `%`). Move System Uptime into the trailing accessory of the Composition or Per-Core section so Details stays within the 3-section cap.
