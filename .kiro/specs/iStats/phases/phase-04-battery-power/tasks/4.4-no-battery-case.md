# Task 4.4 — Handle the no-battery case

**Phase:** 4 — Battery & Power
**Requirements:** 8.4

## What to do

- Detect machines with no internal battery (e.g., desktop Macs).
- Hide battery metrics or mark them not-applicable in the UI.

## Goal (definition of done)

- On a no-battery machine, battery metrics are hidden / marked not-applicable.
- No misleading zero or error is shown.
