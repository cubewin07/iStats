# Task 4.3 — Instantaneous power draw / wattage

**Phase:** 4 — Battery & Power
**Requirements:** 8.3

## What to do

- Report instantaneous power draw / wattage (system and/or battery) where the hardware
  exposes it.
- Report adapter power when plugged in.
- Mark `.unavailable` where not exposed.

## Goal (definition of done)

- Wattage and adapter power are reported where available.
- Unexposed values degrade to `.unavailable` rather than zero.
