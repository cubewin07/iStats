# Task 4.2 — Battery health metrics

**Phase:** 4 — Battery & Power
**Requirements:** 8.2

## What to do

Read battery health from the `AppleSmartBattery` IOKit registry:

- Cycle count.
- Condition.
- Design capacity vs current maximum capacity.

## Goal (definition of done)

- Cycle count, condition, and design-vs-current capacity are reported.
- Values match System Information's battery details.
