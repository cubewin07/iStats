# Task 6.3 — Persist all preferences across launches

**Phase:** 6 — Polish, Preferences & Performance
**Requirements:** 11.4

## What to do

- Ensure every preference (categories, menu bar content, units, interval, launch-at-login,
  Dock icon) is persisted and restored on launch.

## Goal (definition of done)

- Quitting and relaunching restores all preferences exactly.
- Only preferences are persisted — never telemetry (ADR 0006).
