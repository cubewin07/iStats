# Task 1.6 — Build the preferences shell

**Phase:** 1 — Foundation
**Requirements:** 11.2, 11.4

## What to do

- Build a SwiftUI preferences window backed by a persisted settings store.
- Wire the refresh-interval control with sane min/max bounds.

## Goal (definition of done)

- Changing the refresh interval persists across launches (Requirement 11.4).
- Interval values are clamped to the configured min/max bounds (Requirement 11.2).
