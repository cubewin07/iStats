# Task 1.2 — Install the status item and detail popover

**Phase:** 1 — Foundation
**Requirements:** 9.1, 9.3

## What to do

- Install an `NSStatusItem` (via a `MenuBarController`) that shows a placeholder.
- Wire a click on the status item to open an empty detail popover (SwiftUI content).

## Goal (definition of done)

- A status item appears in the menu bar on launch.
- Clicking it toggles an (empty) detail popover open/closed.
